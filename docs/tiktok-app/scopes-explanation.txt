Clipador is a desktop app I run on my own computer to post short clips of streamers who authorize clipping to my own TikTok accounts. Login Kit (user.info.basic): I click "Connect TikTok" in Clipador, log in and authorize; the app shows the connected account's display name so I know which profile will receive the posts. Content Posting API, Direct Post (video.publish): after I approve a clip on the review screen, Clipador queries creator_info, uploads the MP4 with FILE_UPLOAD and publishes it with the caption (credit to the original streamer + hashtags) and the privacy level I chose, then checks the publish status. Nothing is posted without my approval. video.list: Clipador reads views, likes and comments of the videos it published, to show which clips performed best and improve which moments get clipped. Tokens stay only on my computer.
